Privacy Policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

Workout Scribe doesn't collect any data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the developer can't see your workouts.

Where your data lives

Workouts, templates, exercises and settings are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, they also sync through your own private iCloud account, which only you can read; it counts against your iCloud storage. Your profile photo stays on your device.

Purchases

Tips and monthly support are handled by Apple. The app only learns whether a purchase went through, to show your supporter badge and icons.

Notifications

Rest-timer alerts are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Deleting your data

Delete workouts in History, or delete the app to remove everything on the device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Workout Scribe.

Health information

Recovery, fatigue and strength figures are estimates for training, not medical advice.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see Support.
