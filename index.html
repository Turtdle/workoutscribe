A free, private workout log for iPhone. No ads, no account, no tracking.

  • Log sets fast, with a rest timer on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • Gold checks when a set beats what last time predicts, adjusted for bench angle and fatigue.
  • Track incline bench angles, muscle recovery by training split, PRs and progress charts.
  • Plate calculator, warm-up sets, home-screen widgets and iCloud sync.
  • Import your history from Strong.
