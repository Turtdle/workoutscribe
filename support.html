Common questions

How do I move over from Strong?
In Strong, go to Settings → Export Strong Data and save the CSV to Files. In Workout Scribe, open Profile → Import from Strong and choose the file.

Does it sync between my devices?
Yes, through your own iCloud account, with no login. Check Profile → Settings → iCloud Sync.

Why didn't the rest timer buzz?
Allow notifications for Workout Scribe in the iPhone Settings app. The alert is silent and only vibrates.

What weight do I enter?
Barbells include the bar (135 lb = 45 lb bar + 45 + 45). Machines count only the weight you add. Tap the ⓘ next to the weight label in a workout.

How do I cancel monthly support?
Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Workout Scribe.
